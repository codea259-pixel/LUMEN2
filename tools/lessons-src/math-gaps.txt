lesson: Perpendicular vectors and angles

num2: What is the angle, in degrees, between the vectors (2, 2) and (3, 0)? | 45

num2: Vector u has magnitude 4, vector v has magnitude 5, and the angle between them is 60 degrees. What is the dot product of u and v? | 10

lesson: 2 by 2 matrices and determinants
num1: What is the determinant of [[3, 1], [2, 5]]? | 13
hint: For [[a, b], [c, d]], the determinant is ad - bc.
num1: What is the determinant of [[4, -2], [3, 1]]? | 10
hint: Find ad and bc first. Subtracting a negative number adds it.
num1: What is the determinant of [[-2, 5], [3, -4]]? | -7
hint: Multiply the main diagonal (top left times bottom right), then subtract the other diagonal's product.
mc2: A student says the determinant of [[2, 7], [1, 3]] is 2 x 3 + 7 x 1 = 13. What is the correct determinant? | -1 | 13 | 1 | -13
hint: The two diagonal products are subtracted, not added. Which product comes first?
num2: For what value of k is the determinant of [[k, 4], [3, 6]] equal to 0? | 2
hint: Write ad - bc using k, set it equal to 0, and solve.
mc2: What is [[1, 2], [3, 4]] + [[5, 0], [-1, 2]]? | [[6, 2], [2, 6]] | [[5, 0], [-3, 8]] | [[6, 2], [4, 6]] | [[-4, 2], [4, 2]]
hint: Add the entries in matching positions. Watch the sign of the -1.

lesson: Using matrices and determinants

mc3: The system 2x + 3y = 5 and 4x + 6y = 9 has coefficient matrix [[2, 3], [4, 6]], whose determinant is 0. What can you conclude? | the system does not have exactly one solution | the system has exactly one solution | the only solution is x = 0, y = 0 | the solution is x = 5, y = 9
hint: A zero determinant means the two lines are parallel or the same line.
num3: The area of the parallelogram formed by the vectors (3, 1) and (1, 2) equals abs(det [[3, 1], [1, 2]]). What is the area? | 5

num3: The matrix [[2, 1], [0, 3]] is multiplied by the column vector (4, 5). What is the first component of the result? | 13

num3: The matrix [[2, 0], [0, 3]] stretches every shape in the plane. A square with area 4 is transformed by this matrix. What is the area of the new shape? | 24

mc3: What is the matrix product [[1, 2], [0, 1]] x [[3, 0], [1, 2]]? | [[5, 4], [1, 2]] | [[3, 0], [0, 2]] | [[3, 6], [1, 4]] | [[4, 2], [1, 3]]

num1: A class of 50 students forms teams of 6. How many students are left over? | 2

lesson: Probability and mixed practice
