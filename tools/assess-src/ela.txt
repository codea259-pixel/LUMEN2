@el 6-8 | Allow a bilingual glossary of literary terms (theme, claim, evidence, tone). Offer a paragraph frame (claim, evidence, explanation) for written responses.
@el 9-12 | Provide a word bank of academic vocabulary for the unit and allow students to plan writing in their home language before drafting in English.

@iep 9-12 | Extended time, text-to-speech for passages, an outline template for essays, and reduced length on the performance task where the IEP allows.
