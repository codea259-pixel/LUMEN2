about: Transitional kindergarten history-social science follows the California Preschool Learning Foundations. Children learn about themselves, their families and cultures, how to be fair and kind members of a class community, the ideas of past, present and future, the places around them and how to care for them, and how people work, trade and help each other. All items are meant to be read aloud, and pictures and maps are described in words.
std: PK.HSS.1 | Self and Society: understand self, family, culture and diversity, relationships, and the roles and jobs of people in the community.
std: PK.HSS.2 | Becoming a Preschool Community Member (Civics): follow group rules, take responsibility, show fairness and respect for others, and solve conflicts with words.
std: PK.HSS.3 | Sense of Time (History): understand past events, anticipate future events, tell about personal history, and notice changes in people and places over time.
std: PK.HSS.4 | Sense of Place (Geography and Ecology): find the way in familiar places, care for the natural world, and use simple drawings and maps to show places.
std: PK.HSS.5 | Marketplace (Economics): understand that people trade, buy and sell goods and services, and that people make choices about what they need and want.
