# Grade 6 Social Studies (format 2): World History and Geography: Ancient Civilizations.
# Units follow the California History-Social Science content standards 6.1 to 6.7 in order.
@subject soc
@grade 6
@program California History-Social Science Framework
about: Sixth graders study the ancient world, from the first humans to the fall of Rome: how people began farming, and how the civilizations of Mesopotamia, Egypt and Kush, ancient Israel, Greece, India, China and Rome developed their governments, economies, beliefs and ideas. Students read maps, timelines and primary sources, explain cause and effect, compare perspectives and write claims supported by evidence.
std: 6.1 | Students describe what is known through archaeological studies of the early physical and cultural development of humankind from the Paleolithic era to the agricultural revolution.
std: 6.2 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the early civilizations of Mesopotamia, Egypt and Kush.
std: 6.3 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the Ancient Hebrews.
std: 6.4 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the early civilizations of Ancient Greece.
std: 6.5 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the early civilizations of India.
std: 6.6 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the early civilizations of China.
std: 6.7 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures during the development of Rome.
std: HI 1 | Students explain the central issues and problems from the past, placing people and events in a matter of time and place, and use evidence from primary and secondary sources.
