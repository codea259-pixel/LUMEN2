@subject sci
@grade TK
@program Mystery Science
about: Transitional kindergarten science helps children wonder, observe and explore with their senses and simple tools. Children notice how objects and materials look, feel and change, try pushes and pulls, care for plants and animals and watch them grow, and observe rocks, water, weather and the sky. Items are read aloud and many are answered by pointing, saying or drawing.
std: PK.SCI.1 | Observation and investigation: use the senses and simple tools (magnifier, measuring cup, scale) to observe, compare, sort, predict and try things out.
std: PK.SCI.2 | Documentation and communication: record observations with drawings, marks and simple charts, and share what was seen and learned.
std: PK.SCI.3 | Properties of nonliving objects and materials: describe and compare size, shape, color, texture, weight and what materials are made of.
std: PK.SCI.4 | Changes in nonliving objects and materials: observe and describe how objects move (push, pull, roll, slide) and how materials change (mix, melt, freeze, get wet or dry).
std: PK.SCI.5 | Properties of living things: identify living things, their body parts and what plants, animals and people need to live, and where animals live.
std: PK.SCI.6 | Changes in living things: observe and describe how plants and animals, including people, grow and change over time.
std: PK.SCI.7 | Properties of earth materials: observe and describe rocks, soil, sand and water.
std: PK.SCI.8 | Changes in the earth: observe and describe weather, seasons, day and night, and the Sun, Moon and stars.
