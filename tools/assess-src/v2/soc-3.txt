# Grade 3 Social Studies, format 2. Follows the California History-Social Science Framework, grade 3: Continuity and Change.
@subject soc
@grade 3
@program California History-Social Science Framework
about: Third grade, Continuity and Change, studies the local region: its land and water, the California Indian nations who have lived there for thousands of years, the explorers and newcomers who changed it, the rules, laws and governments that hold communities together, and the local economy. Students read maps, timelines and primary sources and compare the views of different people.
std: 3.1 | Students describe the physical and human geography and use maps, tables, graphs, photographs and charts to organize information about people, places and environments in a spatial context.
std: 3.2 | Students describe the American Indian nations in their local region long ago and in the recent past.
std: 3.3 | Students draw from historical and community resources to organize the sequence of local historical events and describe how each period of settlement left its mark on the land.
std: 3.4 | Students understand the role of rules and laws in our daily lives and the basic structure of the U.S. government.
std: 3.5 | Students demonstrate basic economic reasoning skills and an understanding of the economy of the local region.
std: CST | Chronological and spatial thinking: students place events in order, read timelines and use map skills to find locations.
std: REPV | Research, evidence and point of view: students tell primary sources from secondary sources and compare different points of view.
