about: Grade 8 integrated science (California's preferred middle school model) uses phenomena such as collisions, asteroid impacts and mass extinctions to study forces and motion, energy, fields that act at a distance, the solar system, waves and information, the history of life on Earth, and how genes, natural selection and human choices shape species and biodiversity. Students plan investigations, use math and models, analyze data and design solutions.

std: MS-PS2-4 | Construct and present arguments using evidence to support the claim that gravitational interactions are attractive and depend on the masses of interacting objects.
