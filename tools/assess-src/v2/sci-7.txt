about: Grade 7 integrated science (California's preferred middle school model) uses phenomena to show that organisms and nonliving things are made of atoms, that matter cycles and energy flows through organisms, ecosystems and rocks, and that natural processes and human activities shape Earth's surface, resources and biodiversity. Students model particles and reactions, analyze data, construct explanations and design solutions.
std: MS-PS1-1 | Develop models to describe the atomic composition of simple molecules and extended structures.
std: MS-PS1-4 | Develop a model that predicts and describes changes in particle motion, temperature and state of a pure substance when thermal energy is added or removed.
std: MS-PS1-2 | Analyze and interpret data on the properties of substances before and after the substances interact to determine if a chemical reaction has occurred.
std: MS-PS1-5 | Develop and use a model to describe how the total number of atoms does not change in a chemical reaction and thus mass is conserved.
std: MS-LS1-6 | Construct a scientific explanation based on evidence for the role of photosynthesis in the cycling of matter and flow of energy into and out of organisms.
std: MS-LS2-3 | Develop a model to describe the cycling of matter and flow of energy among living and nonliving parts of an ecosystem.

std: MS-ESS2-3 | Analyze and interpret data on the distribution of fossils and rocks, continental shapes and seafloor structures to provide evidence of the past plate motions.
