about: First grade grows confident readers and writers around knowledge topics from science and social studies: decoding short and long vowel words, digraphs, blends, vowel teams, r-controlled vowels and two-syllable words; reading high-frequency words; retelling stories and finding the central message; finding the main topic and key details in information texts; and writing short opinion, informative and narrative pieces in complete sentences with correct capitals, end marks and grade 1 grammar. Passages and questions are read aloud to students as needed.
std: RL.1.2 | Retell stories, including key details, and demonstrate understanding of their central message or lesson.
std: RL.1.3 | Describe characters, settings and major events in a story, using key details.
std: RI.1.2 | Identify the main topic and retell key details of a text.
std: RF.1.2 | Demonstrate understanding of spoken words, syllables and sounds: blend and segment the sounds in single-syllable words, and tell long from short vowels.
std: RF.1.3 | Know and apply grade-level phonics: digraphs, final -e and vowel teams, syllables in two-syllable words, inflectional endings, and irregular high-frequency words.
std: W.1.2 | Write informative texts that name a topic, supply some facts, and provide a sense of closure (with opinion and narrative writing under W.1.1 and W.1.3).
std: W.1.3 | Write narratives that recount two or more sequenced events, include details, use time order words and provide a sense of closure.
std: L.1.1 | Use grade 1 grammar: common, proper and plural nouns, verbs in past, present and future, pronouns, adjectives, conjunctions, prepositions and complete sentences.
