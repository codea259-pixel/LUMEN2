@subject sci
@grade K
@program Mystery Science
about: Kindergarten science asks children to investigate and wonder about everyday phenomena: how pushes and pulls change the way things move, how sunlight warms Earth's surface, how weather changes from day to day and season to season, what plants and animals (including people) need to survive, how living things change the places they live, and how people can take care of land, water, air and living things. Children plan simple tests, observe, record with drawings and charts, and design simple solutions. Items are read aloud.
std: K-PS2-1 | Plan and conduct an investigation to compare the effects of different strengths or different directions of pushes and pulls on the motion of an object.
std: K-PS3-1 | Make observations to determine the effect of sunlight on Earth's surface.
std: K-LS1-1 | Use observations to describe patterns of what plants and animals (including humans) need to survive.
std: K-ESS2-1 | Use and share observations of local weather conditions to describe patterns over time.
std: K-ESS2-2 | Construct an argument supported by evidence for how plants and animals (including humans) can change the environment to meet their needs.
std: K-ESS3-1 | Use a model to represent the relationship between the needs of different plants or animals (including humans) and the places they live.
std: K-ESS3-3 | Communicate solutions that will reduce the impact of humans on the land, water, air and/or other living things in the local environment.
std: K-2-ETS1-1 | Ask questions, make observations and gather information about a situation people want to change to define a simple problem that can be solved through a new or improved object or tool.
