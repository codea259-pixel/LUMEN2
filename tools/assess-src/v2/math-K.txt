@program Eureka Math
about: Kindergarten follows the Eureka Math Kindergarten modules: counting and writing numbers to 10, naming and describing flat and solid shapes, comparing lengths, weights, capacities and numbers, finding number pairs and solving addition and subtraction stories within 10, understanding teen numbers as ten ones and some more ones, counting to 100, and building and composing shapes. Items are short and meant to be read aloud.
std: K.CC.A.1 | Count to 100 by ones and by tens.
std: K.CC.B.4 | Understand the relationship between numbers and quantities; the last number said tells how many, and each next number is one more.
std: K.CC.B.5 | Count to answer "how many?" questions about as many as 20 things in a line, array or circle, or 10 things scattered.
std: K.CC.C.6 | Identify whether the number of objects in one group is greater than, less than or equal to the number in another group.
std: K.OA.A.2 | Solve addition and subtraction word problems within 10 using objects or drawings.
std: K.OA.A.3 | Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, and record each with a drawing or equation.
std: K.NBT.A.1 | Compose and decompose numbers from 11 to 19 into ten ones and some further ones.
std: K.G.B.4 | Analyze and compare two- and three-dimensional shapes using informal language to describe their parts and attributes.
