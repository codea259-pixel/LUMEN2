about: Eleventh grade, United States History and Geography: Continuity and Change in the Twentieth Century, studies industrialization and the Progressive Era, America's rise as a world power, the 1920s and Great Depression, World War II, the Cold War, the civil rights movement and contemporary America.
std: 11.2 | Students analyze the relationship among the rise of industrialization, large-scale rural-to-urban migration and massive immigration from Southern and Eastern Europe.
std: 11.5 | Students analyze the major political, social, economic, technological and cultural developments of the 1920s.
std: 11.6 | Students analyze the different explanations for the Great Depression and how the New Deal fundamentally changed the role of the federal government.
std: 11.7 | Students analyze America's participation in World War II.
std: 11.10 | Students analyze the development of federal civil rights and voting rights.
