# Grade 8 Social Studies (format 2): United States History and Geography: Growth and Conflict.
# Units follow the California History-Social Science content standards 8.1 to 8.12 in order.
@subject soc
@grade 8
@program California History-Social Science Framework
about: Eighth graders study the United States from the Revolution to 1914: the ideas of the founding, the Constitution and Bill of Rights, the early republic, the growth of the North, South and West, slavery and the abolition movement, the Civil War, Reconstruction and the rise of industry and immigration. Students analyze primary sources, compare perspectives, explain cause and effect and write evidence-based arguments.
std: 8.1 | Students understand the major events preceding the founding of the nation and relate their significance to the development of American constitutional democracy.
std: 8.2 | Students analyze the political principles underlying the U.S. Constitution and compare the enumerated and implied powers of the federal government.
std: 8.3 | Students understand the foundation of the American political system and the ways in which citizens participate in it.
std: 8.4 | Students analyze the aspirations and ideals of the people of the new nation.
std: 8.5 | Students analyze U.S. foreign policy in the early Republic.
std: 8.6 | Students analyze the divergent paths of the American people from 1800 to the mid-1800s and the challenges they faced, with emphasis on the Northeast.
std: 8.7 | Students analyze the divergent paths of the American people in the South from 1800 to the mid-1800s and the challenges they faced.
std: 8.8 | Students analyze the divergent paths of the American people in the West from 1800 to the mid-1800s and the challenges they faced.
std: 8.9 | Students analyze the early and steady attempts to abolish slavery and to realize the ideals of the Declaration of Independence.
std: 8.10 | Students analyze the multiple causes, key events and complex consequences of the Civil War.
std: 8.11 | Students analyze the character and lasting consequences of Reconstruction.
std: 8.12 | Students analyze the transformation of the American economy and the changing social and political conditions in the United States in response to the Industrial Revolution.
