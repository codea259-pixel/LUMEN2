@subject sci
@grade 2
@program Mystery Science
about: Second grade science investigates the properties of materials and how they are chosen for a job, how objects can be built from small pieces, and how heating and cooling cause changes that can or cannot be reversed. Students test what plants need, explore how animals spread seeds and pollinate flowers, compare the diversity of living things in different habitats, observe Earth events that happen quickly or slowly, compare solutions that slow erosion by wind and water, and use maps and models to describe land and water on Earth. Items are read aloud as needed.
std: 2-PS1-1 | Plan and conduct an investigation to describe and classify different kinds of materials by their observable properties.
std: 2-PS1-2 | Analyze data obtained from testing different materials to determine which materials have the properties that are best suited for an intended purpose.
std: 2-PS1-4 | Construct an argument with evidence that some changes caused by heating or cooling can be reversed and some cannot.
std: 2-LS2-1 | Plan and conduct an investigation to determine if plants need sunlight and water to grow.
std: 2-LS2-2 | Develop a simple model that mimics the function of an animal in dispersing seeds or pollinating plants.
std: 2-LS4-1 | Make observations of plants and animals to compare the diversity of life in different habitats.
std: 2-ESS1-1 | Use information from several sources to provide evidence that Earth events can occur quickly or slowly.
std: 2-ESS2-1 | Compare multiple solutions designed to slow or prevent wind or water from changing the shape of the land.
