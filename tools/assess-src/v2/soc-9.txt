about: Ninth grade social studies combines World Geography and Ethnic Studies (California does not set one required ninth-grade course): students use spatial thinking to study Earth's regions, population and migration, culture and globalization, and the histories, identities and movements of communities of color in the United States.
std: HSS.CS.3 | Use a variety of maps and documents to interpret human movement, including major patterns of domestic and international migration and changing environmental preferences.
std: HSS.CS.4 | Relate current events to the physical and human characteristics of places and regions.
std: HSS.REP.4 | Assess the credibility of primary and secondary sources and draw sound conclusions from them.
std: ESMC.1 | Analyze how race, ethnicity and identity are socially and historically constructed and how they shape experience.
std: ESMC.2 | Examine histories of resistance, movements and contributions of African American, Chicana/o and Latina/o, Native American and Asian American and Pacific Islander communities.
