about: First grade follows the Eureka Math Grade 1 modules: sums and differences to 10, making ten and taking from ten within 20, measuring and comparing lengths with units, place value and comparison to 40 and then to 100, adding two-digit numbers, describing, composing and partitioning shapes, and telling time to the half hour. Items are short and meant to be read aloud.
std: 1.OA.A.1 | Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems with unknowns in all positions, using objects, drawings and equations.
std: 1.OA.C.6 | Add and subtract within 20, fluently within 10, using strategies such as counting on, making ten and decomposing a number leading to ten.
std: 1.OA.D.8 | Determine the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation relating three whole numbers.
std: 1.NBT.B.2 | Understand that the two digits of a two-digit number represent amounts of tens and ones.
std: 1.NBT.B.3 | Compare two two-digit numbers based on the tens and ones digits, recording the results with >, = and <.
std: 1.NBT.C.4 | Add within 100 using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on place value, including adding a two-digit number and a one-digit number or a multiple of 10.
std: 1.MD.A.2 | Express the length of an object as a whole number of same-size length units laid end to end with no gaps or overlaps.
std: 1.G.A.3 | Partition circles and rectangles into two and four equal shares and describe them as halves, fourths and quarters.
