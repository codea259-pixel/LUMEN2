@program California History-Social Science Framework
about: First grade history-social science, A Child's Place in Time and Space, helps children learn about rights, rules and responsibilities, find their place on maps and globes, know the symbols, songs and holidays of the United States, compare life now and long ago, appreciate the many cultures that make up their California community, and understand money, work and trade. Items are meant to be read aloud, and maps, pictures, timelines and objects are described in words.
std: 1.1 | Students describe the rights and individual responsibilities of citizenship, including how rules are made and why we respect the rights of others.
std: 1.2 | Students compare and contrast the absolute and relative locations of places and people and describe the physical and human characteristics of places.
std: 1.3 | Students know and understand the symbols, icons and traditions of the United States that provide continuity and a sense of community across time.
std: 1.4 | Students compare and contrast everyday life in different times and places around the world and recognize that some aspects of people, places and things change over time while others stay the same.
std: 1.5 | Students describe the human characteristics of familiar places and the varied backgrounds of American citizens and residents in those places.
std: 1.6 | Students understand basic economic concepts and the role of individual choice in a free-market economy.
