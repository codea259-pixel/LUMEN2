# ELA Transitional Kindergarten (format 2). Original items written to the California Preschool Learning Foundations
# (Language and Literacy). Units follow a Benchmark Advance-style year of knowledge topics; titles are our own.
@subject ela
@grade TK
@program Benchmark Advance
about: Transitional kindergarten builds oral language and early literacy through read-aloud stories and information books: listening and retelling, answering questions, rhyming and beginning sounds, naming letters (starting with the letters in each child's name), concepts of print, new vocabulary, and drawing, marks and dictation to share ideas. Every item is read aloud to children.
std: PK.RL.1 | With support, listen to stories, answer questions about them, and retell familiar stories with characters and events.
std: PK.RI.1 | With support, listen to information books and tell facts and details learned from them.
std: PK.RF.1 | Recognize and name upper- and lowercase letters, especially those in the child's own name, and connect some letters to their sounds.
std: PK.RF.2 | Hear and play with sounds: rhyming words, syllables (word parts) and beginning sounds.
std: PK.RF.3 | Understand concepts of print: books are read front to back, print is read left to right and top to bottom, and words carry meaning.
std: PK.SL.1 | Take turns in conversation, listen to others, and use new words to describe objects, actions and feelings.
std: PK.L.1 | Speak in complete sentences and use describing words, action words and position words (in, on, under).
std: PK.W.1 | Use drawing, marks, letter-like shapes and some letters, and dictation, to share ideas and tell about experiences.
