about: Second grade readers decode longer words with vowel teams, prefixes and suffixes, retell stories, fables and folktales and find their central message, describe how characters respond to events, and use text features to find the main topic and key details of informational texts. Ten knowledge units (about three weeks each) pair a science or social studies topic with a reading focus and a writing focus: narrative, informative and opinion writing, plus grammar and conventions.
std: RF.2.3 | Know and apply grade-level phonics and word analysis skills in decoding words, including long vowels, vowel teams, prefixes and suffixes.
std: RL.2.2 | Recount stories, including fables and folktales from diverse cultures, and determine their central message, lesson or moral.
std: RL.2.3 | Describe how characters in a story respond to major events and challenges.
std: RI.2.2 | Identify the main topic of a multiparagraph text as well as the focus of specific paragraphs within the text.
std: RI.2.5 | Know and use various text features (captions, bold print, subheadings, glossaries, indexes) to locate key facts or information efficiently.
std: W.2.2 | Write informative/explanatory texts that introduce a topic, use facts and definitions to develop points, and provide a concluding statement.
std: L.2.1 | Demonstrate command of the conventions of standard English grammar and usage, including collective nouns, irregular plurals, irregular past tense verbs, adjectives and adverbs.
std: L.2.4 | Determine or clarify the meaning of unknown words and phrases using context clues, prefixes, root words and compound words.
