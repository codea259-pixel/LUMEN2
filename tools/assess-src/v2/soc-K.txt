about: Kindergarten history-social science, Learning and Working Now and Long Ago, helps children become good citizens in their classroom, recognize symbols of the United States and California, match jobs to the work people do, use position words and simple maps, put events in order on a calendar, and learn how people lived long ago and whom we honor on holidays. Items are meant to be read aloud, and maps, pictures and objects are described in words.
std: K.1 | Students understand that being a good citizen involves acting in certain ways: following rules, sharing, taking turns, and learning examples of honesty, courage, determination and responsibility.
std: K.2 | Students recognize national and state symbols and icons such as the national and state flags, the bald eagle and the Statue of Liberty.
std: K.3 | Students match simple descriptions of work that people do and the names of related jobs at school, in the local community and from historical accounts.
std: K.4 | Students compare and contrast the locations of people, places and environments and describe their characteristics, using position words, maps, globes and map symbols.
std: K.5 | Students put events in temporal order using a calendar, placing days, weeks and months in proper order.
std: K.6 | Students understand that history relates to events, people and places of other times, including holidays, stories of people from the past, and daily life long ago.
