# ELA Kindergarten (format 2). Original items written to the California Common Core State Standards for ELA.
# Units follow a Benchmark Advance-style year of knowledge topics; titles are our own. Teachers match units to their pacing guide.
@subject ela
@grade K
@program Benchmark Advance
about: Kindergarten builds the foundations of reading and writing around knowledge topics from science and social studies: concepts of print, letter names and sounds, rhyming, syllables and sounds in words, decoding short-vowel (CVC) words and reading high-frequency words, asking and answering questions about read-aloud stories and information texts, and using drawing, dictating and writing to share opinions, information and events. Every item is read aloud to students.
std: RL.K.1 | With prompting and support, ask and answer questions about key details in a text, including characters, settings and major events.
std: RI.K.2 | With prompting and support, identify the main topic and retell key details of a text.
std: RF.K.1 | Demonstrate understanding of the organization and basic features of print, and recognize and name all upper- and lowercase letters.
std: RF.K.2 | Demonstrate understanding of spoken words, syllables and sounds (phonemes): rhyme, syllables, onset-rime, and the sounds in CVC words.
std: RF.K.3 | Know and apply grade-level phonics: letter-sound correspondences, short and long vowel sounds, and common high-frequency words.
std: W.K.2 | Use a combination of drawing, dictating and writing to compose opinion, informative and narrative pieces.
std: L.K.1 | Print letters, use nouns and verbs, form plural nouns with -s, use question words and prepositions, and speak in complete sentences.
std: L.K.5 | With guidance, sort words into categories, understand opposites, and connect words to their use in real life.
