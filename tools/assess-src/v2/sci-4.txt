# Grade 4 Science (California NGSS), format 2. Units are original and grouped by topic for classes using Mystery Science.
@subject sci
@grade 4
@program Mystery Science
about: Fourth graders investigate energy: how speed relates to energy, what happens in collisions, and how energy moves by sound, light, heat and electric current. They design devices that convert energy, study natural resources, model waves and the way light lets us see, explore how plant and animal structures and senses support survival, and read rock layers and landforms to explain how Earth's surface changes and how people reduce the impact of natural hazards.
std: 4-PS3-1 | Use evidence to construct an explanation relating the speed of an object to the energy of that object.
std: 4-PS3-2 | Make observations to provide evidence that energy can be transferred from place to place by sound, light, heat and electric currents.
std: 4-PS3-4 | Apply scientific ideas to design, test and refine a device that converts energy from one form to another.
std: 4-PS4-1 | Develop a model of waves to describe patterns in terms of amplitude and wavelength and that waves can cause objects to move.
std: 4-LS1-1 | Construct an argument that plants and animals have internal and external structures that function to support survival, growth, behavior and reproduction.
std: 4-ESS1-1 | Identify evidence from patterns in rock formations and fossils in rock layers to support an explanation for changes in a landscape over time.
std: 4-ESS3-1 | Obtain and combine information to describe that energy and fuels are derived from natural resources and their uses affect the environment.
std: 4-ESS3-2 | Generate and compare multiple solutions to reduce the impacts of natural Earth processes on humans.

lesson: Why does a faster object have more energy? | 4-PS3-1
practice:

hint: Think about what the height gave the marble before it was released.
num2: A marble rolled 1 meter in 4 seconds from a low ramp and 1 meter in 2 seconds from a high ramp. How many seconds faster was the high-ramp roll? | 2
