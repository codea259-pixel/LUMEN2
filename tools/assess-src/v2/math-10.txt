lesson: Rigid motions: translations, reflections and rotations | G-CO.A.5, G-CO.A.2
practice:
mc1: The point (3, 5) is translated 4 units left and 2 units down. Where does it land? | (-1, 3) | (7, 3) | (-1, 7) | (1, 1)
hint: Left changes x; down changes y.
mc2: Reflect the point (4, -1) across the x-axis. | (4, 1) | (-4, -1) | (-4, 1) | (-1, 4)
hint: A reflection across the x-axis keeps x the same.
mc2: Rotate the point (2, 5) 90 degrees counterclockwise about the origin. | (-5, 2) | (5, -2) | (-2, -5) | (5, 2)
hint: A 90-degree counterclockwise rotation sends (x, y) to (-y, x).
mc2: Reflect the point (6, 1) across the line y = x. | (1, 6) | (-6, 1) | (6, -1) | (-1, -6)
hint: A reflection across y = x swaps the coordinates.
num2: A segment from (0, 0) to (6, 8) is rotated 90 degrees about the origin. How long is the image segment? | 10
hint: Rigid motions preserve length. Find the length of the original segment.
sa2: Explain which properties of a figure stay the same under every rigid motion. | Lengths, angle measures, parallel lines and area stay the same. Only the position and orientation can change, so the image is congruent to the original.

lesson: Congruence through rigid motions | G-CO.B.6, G-CO.B.7

sa2: Explain how to show that the triangle with vertices A(0, 0), B(2, 0), C(0, 3) is congruent to the triangle with vertices A'(5, 1), B'(7, 1), C'(5, 4). | A translation 5 right and 1 up maps A to A', B to B' and C to C'. Translations preserve lengths and angles, so the triangles are congruent.

mc3: Triangle A(1, 1), B(4, 1), C(1, 3) maps to A'(-1, -1), B'(-4, -1), C'(-1, -3). Which single rigid motion does this? | A rotation of 180 degrees about the origin | A reflection across the x-axis | A reflection across the y-axis | A translation 2 left and 2 down
sa2: Explain why two triangles whose corresponding sides and angles are all equal are congruent under the rigid motion definition. | Translate one triangle so a pair of matching vertices meet, rotate so a matching side lines up, and reflect if needed. Because all the sides and angles match, the third vertices land on each other, so a sequence of rigid motions maps one triangle onto the other.

lesson: Triangle congruence criteria: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS and HL | G-CO.B.8, G-CO.B.7
practice:
mc1: Two triangles have all three pairs of sides congruent. Which criterion proves them congruent? | SSS | SAS | ASA | HL
hint: Count what is given: sides only.
mc2: AB = DE, angle B = angle E and BC = EF. Which criterion proves triangle ABC congruent to triangle DEF? | SAS | SSS | ASA | AAS
hint: Is the angle between the two sides?
mc2: Angle A = angle D, angle B = angle E and BC = EF. Which criterion proves triangle ABC congruent to triangle DEF? | AAS | ASA | SAS | SSA
hint: Is the given side between the two given angles?
mc2: Two right triangles have congruent hypotenuses and one pair of congruent legs. Which criterion proves them congruent? | HL | SSA | AAA | SAS
hint: This criterion works only for right triangles.
mc2: Which information is NOT enough to prove two triangles congruent? | Three pairs of congruent angles | Two pairs of sides and the included angle | Three pairs of sides | Two pairs of angles and the included side
hint: Two triangles can have the same angles but different sizes.
sa2: Explain why ASA proves two triangles congruent, using rigid motions. | Use rigid motions to place the matching included sides on top of each other. The angles at both ends of that side match, so the other two sides leave along the same rays and meet at the same point. The third vertices coincide, so the triangles are congruent.
quiz:
mc1: Which is a valid triangle congruence criterion? | ASA | SSA | AAA | AA
mc2: In triangles PQR and STU, PQ = ST, QR = TU and PR = SU. Which statement is proved? | Triangle PQR is congruent to triangle STU by SSS | The triangles are similar but not congruent | Triangle PQR is congruent to triangle UTS by SAS | Nothing can be proved
mc2: Segments AC and BD bisect each other at point E. Which criterion proves triangle AEB congruent to triangle CED? | SAS | SSS | ASA | HL
mc2: M is the midpoint of segment XY, and segment ZM is perpendicular to XY. Which criterion proves triangle XMZ congruent to triangle YMZ? | SAS | AAA | SSA | HL
num2: Triangle ABC is congruent to triangle DEF by SSS. AB = 2x - 1 and DE = x + 6. What is the length of AB? | 13
num2: Two triangles are congruent by ASA. One triangle has angles of 35 and 80 degrees. What is the third angle of the other triangle in degrees? | 65
mc3: Kite ABCD has AB = AD and CB = CD, and diagonal AC is drawn. Which criterion proves triangle ABC congruent to triangle ADC? | SSS | SAS | ASA | HL
sa3: Sofia says two triangles are congruent whenever two pairs of sides and one pair of angles are congruent. Explain when she is right and when she is not. | She is right when the angle is between the two sides (SAS). If the angle is not between them (SSA), two different triangles can fit the information, so congruence is not guaranteed. The exception is right triangles with a congruent hypotenuse and leg (HL).
