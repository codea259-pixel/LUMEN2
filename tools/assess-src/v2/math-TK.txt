@program Eureka Math
about: Transitional kindergarten follows the Eureka Math Pre-Kindergarten modules: counting to 5 and then to 10 and 20, naming shapes and position words, comparing length, weight, capacity and groups, and acting out simple addition and subtraction stories with real objects. Items are short and meant to be read aloud; objects are described in words.
std: PK.NS.1 | Recite numbers in order to 20 and count up to 10 objects, saying one number for each object; the last number tells how many.
std: PK.NS.2 | Recognize and name numerals 0 to 10 and match them to groups of objects.
std: PK.NS.3 | Compare two groups of up to 5 objects by matching and counting and tell which has more, fewer or the same.
std: PK.NO.1 | Understand that adding to or taking away from a group changes the number; act out and solve simple stories within 5.
std: PK.AF.1 | Sort and classify objects by one attribute, such as color, size or kind.
std: PK.M.1 | Compare two objects by length, weight or capacity using words such as longer, heavier and holds more.
std: PK.G.1 | Identify, describe and build shapes, including circles, triangles, squares, rectangles, spheres, cubes and cylinders.
std: PK.G.2 | Describe where objects are using position words such as above, below, next to, in front of and behind.
