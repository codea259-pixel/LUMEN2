# Grade 7 Social Studies (format 2): World History and Geography: Medieval and Early Modern Times.
# Units follow the California History-Social Science content standards 7.1 to 7.11 in order.
@subject soc
@grade 7
@program California History-Social Science Framework
about: Seventh graders study the world from about 300 to 1789 CE: the fall of Rome and the Byzantine Empire, the rise and spread of Islam, China, the West African empires, Japan, medieval Europe, the Maya, Aztec and Inca, and the Renaissance, Reformation, Scientific Revolution, Age of Exploration and Enlightenment. Students trace trade and the spread of ideas, read primary sources, compare perspectives and explain causes and effects with evidence.
std: 7.1 | Students analyze the causes and effects of the vast expansion and ultimate disintegration of the Roman Empire.
std: 7.2 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the civilizations of Islam in the Middle Ages.
std: 7.3 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the civilizations of China in the Middle Ages.
std: 7.4 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the sub-Saharan civilizations of Ghana and Mali in Medieval Africa.
std: 7.5 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the civilizations of Medieval Japan.
std: 7.6 | Students analyze the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the civilizations of Medieval Europe.
std: 7.7 | Students compare and contrast the geographic, political, economic, religious and social structures of the Meso-American and Andean civilizations.
std: 7.8 | Students analyze the origins, accomplishments and geographic diffusion of the Renaissance.
std: 7.9 | Students analyze the historical developments of the Reformation.
std: 7.10 | Students analyze the historical developments of the Scientific Revolution and its lasting effect on religious, political and cultural institutions.
std: 7.11 | Students analyze political and economic change in the sixteenth, seventeenth and eighteenth centuries (the Age of Exploration, the Enlightenment and the Age of Reason).
