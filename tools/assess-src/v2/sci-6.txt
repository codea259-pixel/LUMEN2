about: Grade 6 integrated science (California's preferred middle school model) uses phenomena to study living systems from cells to body systems, how organisms grow and reproduce, thermal energy and its transfer, how energy and water in Earth's systems cause weather and regional climates, and how global warming affects living things. Students plan investigations, analyze data, build models, argue from evidence and design solutions.
std: MS-LS1-1 | Conduct an investigation to provide evidence that living things are made of cells; either one cell or many different numbers and types of cells.
std: MS-LS1-3 | Use argument supported by evidence for how the body is a system of interacting subsystems composed of groups of cells.
std: MS-LS1-5 | Construct a scientific explanation based on evidence for how environmental and genetic factors influence the growth of organisms.
std: MS-PS3-4 | Plan an investigation to determine the relationships among the energy transferred, the type of matter, the mass and the change in the average kinetic energy of the particles as measured by temperature.
std: MS-ESS2-5 | Collect data to provide evidence for how the motions and complex interactions of air masses result in changes in weather conditions.
std: MS-ESS2-6 | Develop and use a model to describe how unequal heating and rotation of the Earth cause patterns of atmospheric and oceanic circulation that determine regional climates.
std: MS-ESS3-5 | Ask questions to clarify evidence of the factors that have caused the rise in global temperatures over the past century.
std: MS-ETS1-2 | Evaluate competing design solutions using a systematic process to determine how well they meet the criteria and constraints of the problem.
