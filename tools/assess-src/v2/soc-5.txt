# Grade 5 Social Studies, format 2. Follows the California History-Social Science Framework, grade 5: United States History and Geography, Making a New Nation.
@subject soc
@grade 5
@program California History-Social Science Framework
about: Fifth grade, United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation, studies the American Indian nations of North America, European exploration, the founding of the thirteen colonies and colonial life, cooperation and conflict between colonists and American Indians, the causes and course of the American Revolution, the writing of the Constitution, and the westward growth of the nation to 1850, along with the fifty states and their capitals. Students read maps, timelines and primary sources and compare the views of American Indians, enslaved and free African Americans, women, colonists and the British.
std: 5.1 | Students describe the major pre-Columbian settlements, including the cliff dwellers and pueblo people of the desert Southwest, the American Indians of the Pacific Northwest, the nomadic nations of the Great Plains and the woodland peoples east of the Mississippi River.
std: 5.2 | Students trace the routes of early explorers and describe the early explorations of the Americas.
std: 5.3 | Students describe the cooperation and conflict that existed among the American Indians and between the Indian nations and the new settlers.
std: 5.4 | Students understand the political, religious, social and economic institutions that evolved in the colonial era.
std: 5.5 | Students explain the causes of the American Revolution.
std: 5.6 | Students understand the course and consequences of the American Revolution.
std: 5.7 | Students describe the people and events associated with the development of the U.S. Constitution and analyze the Constitution's significance as the foundation of the American republic.
std: 5.8 | Students trace the colonization, immigration and settlement patterns of the American people from 1789 to the mid-1800s, and know the locations of the 50 states and their capitals (5.9).
