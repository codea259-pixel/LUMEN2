Student Data Privacy Agreement

between   (the “Local Education Agency” or “LEA”) and Bose Academy (the “Provider”)

About this template. Schools can use this agreement as a starting point and fill in the blanks. Please have it reviewed by your district’s counsel. To start, contact us using the details in our privacy policy.

1. Purpose and scope

The Provider offers Bose Academy (bose.academy), a free online learning service for Pre-K to grade 12: lessons, practice, a knowledge map, teacher class tools, certificates and learning games. This agreement covers student records the Provider receives or creates when the LEA, its teachers or its students use the service for school purposes (“Student Data”). It is written to meet California Education Code section 49073.1, the Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (Business and Professions Code section 22584, “SOPIPA”), the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (“FERPA”) and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (“COPPA”).

2. Student Data the Provider handles

Usernames and passwords (through Google Firebase sign-in); a nickname; grade; whether a student is under 13; learning progress, answers and game progress; class membership, assignments, test answers and scores; certificate names; and, for students who make their own account, a school email address. The Provider does not collect students’ home addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings or precise location. Teacher-made student logins need no student email.

3. Ownership and control (Ed. Code 49073.1(b)(1))

Student Data remains the property of and under the control of the LEA. The Provider uses it only to provide the service to the LEA.

4. Student-generated content (49073.1(b)(2))

Students keep their own work. A student, or a parent for a student, can download everything stored about them as a file from Settings (or the parent page) and keep it, or transfer it to a personal account.

5. No other uses (49073.1(b)(3)); SOPIPA

The Provider will not use Student Data for any purpose other than those required or specifically permitted by this agreement. The Provider will not sell or rent Student Data; use it for targeted advertising; build a profile of a student except to support their learning in the service; or use it to train artificial intelligence models. The service shows no advertising and runs no advertising or analytics trackers.

6. Reviewing and correcting records (49073.1(b)(4))

Parents, legal guardians and eligible students may review Student Data and ask for corrections through the LEA, which can view and change class records in the teacher tools, or directly through the parent page or by contacting the Provider. The Provider will help the LEA respond within the time FERPA allows.

7. Security and confidentiality (49073.1(b)(5))

The Provider protects Student Data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), Google Firebase authentication, database security rules that limit each record to the student, their teacher, their parent and the site owner, and automated tests of those rules. Only the site owner has administrative access. Service providers (Google Firebase for sign-in and data storage, GitHub Pages for hosting) process data only to run the service.

8. Unauthorized disclosure (49073.1(b)(6))

If Student Data is disclosed without authorization, the Provider will notify the LEA without unreasonable delay and no later than 72 hours after confirming it, describe what happened and what data was involved, and help the LEA notify affected parents, legal guardians and eligible students as the law requires.

9. Deletion when the agreement ends (49073.1(b)(7))

When this agreement ends, or earlier at the LEA’s request, the Provider will delete the LEA’s Student Data within 60 days, except data a parent or eligible student has chosen to keep in a personal account, and will confirm the deletion to the LEA in writing. Backups are deleted within 8 weeks.

10. FERPA (49073.1(b)(8))

The Provider acts as a “school official” with a legitimate educational interest under the LEA’s direct control for the use and maintenance of Student Data, and the LEA and the Provider will each comply with FERPA. The Provider will not disclose Student Data except as this agreement or the law allows.

11. Targeted advertising (49073.1(b)(9))

The Provider will not use personally identifiable information in student records to engage in targeted advertising.

12. Children under 13

For students under 13 who use logins the LEA’s teachers create, the LEA consents on parents’ behalf for school use only, as COPPA permits. Students under 13 cannot post in the service’s Community.

13. Term and changes

This agreement starts on the date signed below and lasts for   year(s), or until either party ends it with 30 days’ written notice. The Provider will tell the LEA before making any material change to how it handles Student Data.
