The review covered the public site, the legal pages and the school materials. It is not legal advice. This page tracks
each of its recommendations: who owns it (one of the five agents in `.claude/agents/` and `.claude/skills/conductor/`,
or a person), what has been done, and what still needs a person.

Status key: **Done** (in the repository), **Person** (only the responsible adult can do it), **Ongoing** (a habit, with the tools to support it).

## How to keep this current
- Run `python3 tools/build.py && python3 tools/coverage.py` after content changes. The coverage target is 5 or more lessons for every Learn skill.
- For a goal that spans areas (for example "expand a skill, link it, check it, check privacy"), use the `conductor` skill. It hands the parts to `atlas`, `sentinel`, `compass` and `guardian`, and a person approves before anything goes live.
