Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

The short version

  • Bose Academy is a free learning site for Pre-K to grade 12. We collect only what we need to run it: a username, a nickname, learning progress and, for some people, an email address.
  • We never sell anyone's information, never show ads and never use it to build advertising profiles. There are no advertising or analytics trackers on the site.
  • Children under 13 who sign up themselves need a parent's OK by email first. Until a parent says yes, their progress is saved only on their own device. Children under 13 can't post in the Community.
  • Anyone can download their data or delete their account in Settings. Parents can do the same for their child on the parent page.
  • Questions? Email codea259@gmail.com.

For parents: what we collect, and what we don't

✅ What we collect

  • A username and a nickname (we ask for a nickname, not a real name)
  • Whether the learner is under 13
  • Learning progress: skills practiced, answers, game progress
  • A parent's email, for children under 13 who sign up themselves
  • A school email, for students who make their own account
  • Class work, if a teacher's class is joined
  • Community posts, from users 13 and older only

🚫 What we never collect or do

  • No photos, voice recordings or precise location
  • No home address or phone number from children
  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics trackers
  • We never sell or rent anyone's information
  • We never use learners' information to train AI models
  • Children under 13 can't post in the Community

Your rights: see, download or delete your child's data any time on the parent page, or contact us (details below). The full policy follows.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Bose Academy website and app at bose.academy, including lessons, practice, the knowledge map, games, classes, the Community, certificates and membership. It applies to everyone who uses them: learners of every age, parents and guardians, and teachers.

3. What we collect

When you make an account

  • Username and password. Google Firebase, which runs our sign-in, stores passwords in scrambled form. Nobody at Bose Academy can see them.
  • A nickname (display name) that other people may see. We ask everyone to use a nickname, not a real name.
  • Whether you are a learner, parent or teacher, and whether a learner is under 13.
  • An email address for parents, teachers and anyone who signs in with Google.
  • A school email address for students who make their own account instead of using a login from their teacher. It stays private: only the student, their parent and the site owner can see it, and we use it only to know which school account the student belongs to. Learners with a login made by their teacher don't give an email.
  • A parent's or guardian's email for learners under 13 who sign up themselves, so we can ask for permission (see section 5).

While you learn and play

  • Learning progress: the skills you practice, which answers were right, how long you practiced, your streak and mastery levels.
  • Game progress: experience points, gems, your hero's look, quests and play time.
  • Settings such as text size, high contrast and grade.
  • Reports of a mistake in a question. If you report a question, we keep the question and the reason you picked from a list, but not who sent the report. There is no space to type a message.

In a teacher's class

  • Which classes you belong to, the work your teacher assigns, the answers you turn in, your scores and your teacher's feedback.
  • If the teacher turns on class leaderboards, your nickname, hero look and points are shown to your classmates.
  • If you share a Goblin Gate map you made with your class, your classmates and teacher see the map, its name (picked from word lists, so it has no free text) and your nickname. You or your teacher can remove it at any time, and it is deleted with your account.
  • When a teacher creates logins for students, we keep only each student's first name, a username and a password. No email is needed.

In the Community (13 and older)

  • Posts, replies, votes, reports and study groups. Other signed-in people can see posts and replies, with your nickname.

Certificates

  • When you claim a certificate, the name you type on it, the course and the date. Anyone who has the certificate's verification code can check it on our site.

Membership payments

  • Membership (more game play time) is paid on Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe handles the card details; we never see or store card numbers. We receive the username or class code you enter and a record of the payment, so we can switch membership on. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what you give Stripe.

Information sent automatically

  • Your IP address and browser details reach GitHub, which hosts the site, and Google Firebase, which runs sign-in and our database, because that is how web pages and sign-in work. They use this to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address. Lite mode in Settings turns them off.
  • Storage in your browser. The site saves your progress and settings in your browser, and Firebase uses it to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising cookies.
  • Voice answers (optional). If you tap the microphone to say an answer, your browser turns your speech into text. Some browsers, such as Chrome, do this on their maker's servers. Bose Academy receives only the number you said, never a recording.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for children's full names, home addresses, phone numbers, photos, videos, precise location or contacts. We have no advertising, no analytics trackers and no social media buttons that track you.

4. How we use information

  • To run Bose Academy: saving progress, suggesting what to practice next, games, classes, certificates and membership.
  • To show a learner's progress to that learner, to their teacher (for learners in a class) and to their parent (for children under 13).
  • To keep the Community safe: a word filter, reports from users, and review by verified teachers and the site owner.
  • To keep the site secure and working, and to fix problems.
  • To show public totals on our Impact page, such as the number of questions answered. These are counts only and never identify anyone.

We never: sell or rent personal information; share it for advertising; show ads; build profiles of learners except to support their learning; or use learners' information to train AI models.

Ask Lumi. On bose.academy, the Ask Lumi tutor uses built-in guiding questions, so your questions are not sent to any AI company. If that changes, we will update this policy first and, for children under 13, ask parents.

5. Children under 13

We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Children who sign up themselves

  1. A child under 13 gives us a parent's or guardian's email. We use it only to ask for permission.
  2. We email the parent a sign-in link. It opens a page that explains what we collect and asks the parent to agree or decline.
  3. Until the parent agrees, the child can practice, but their progress is saved only on their own device, they can't claim certificates with their name, and they can only read the Community.
  4. If the parent declines, we delete the child's account and its data. If nobody answers within 14 days, we delete the parent's email.

Children under 13 can never post in the Community. We don't ask a child for more information than they need to use an activity.

Logins made by teachers

Teachers can create logins for students who are under 13 for use at school. In that case the school gives permission for this school use in place of parents, as COPPA allows, and we use the information only for school purposes. Parents can ask the school, or us, to see or delete their child's information.

Parents' rights

At any time, a parent can see their child's progress, download everything we store about their child, delete their child's account and data, or withdraw permission. Use the parent page (we email you a sign-in link) or email us. Deleting the account stops all further collection.

We share children's information only with the service providers that run the site for us (section 8) and when the law requires it. We would ask parents separately before sharing it for any other reason.

6. Students and schools

When Bose Academy is used for school, we follow California's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA) and support schools' duties under FERPA. We use student information only for school purposes. We don't use it for targeted advertising, sell it, build profiles except for school purposes, or share it except as the law allows. We keep it secure, and we delete a school's student information when the school or district asks. Schools and districts can email us to arrange a student data privacy agreement.

10. How we protect information

  • The whole site uses secure HTTPS connections.
  • Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and are never visible to us.
  • Database rules decide who can read or change each record. Automatic tests try to break them every time the site changes.
  • Only one owner account can manage the site, and it signs in with Google.
  • We review our security whenever we add features that handle personal information.

No website is perfectly secure. If information about you is ever exposed, we will tell you, or your parent, as the law requires.

11. Your choices and rights

Everyone, wherever they live, can:

  • See and download their information: Settings, then Download my data.
  • Correct it: change your nickname in the Community, or email us.
  • Delete their account and its data: Settings, then Delete my account. Logins made by a teacher are deleted by the teacher or school. Teachers who have classes email us.
  • Delete their own Community posts and replies with the Delete button.

You can also email us. We answer within 45 days, and we may ask a question to make sure a request really comes from you or your parent. Using these rights never changes how Bose Academy treats you.

California residents

  • Your privacy rights. We honor the rights to know, delete, correct and opt out that California's privacy laws (the CCPA and CPRA) describe. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out.
  • Minors' posts. If you are under 18, you can remove anything you post in the Community with the Delete button, or ask us to remove it (California Business and Professions Code section 22581). Removing a post doesn't remove copies that someone else made.
  • Shine the Light. We don't share personal information with other companies for their direct marketing (California Civil Code section 1798.83).
  • Do Not Track. We don't track people across other websites, and we don't let advertising or analytics companies collect information about your activity on Bose Academy. So we treat every visit the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.
