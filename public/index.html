One loop, from first idea to lasting skill

Other platforms give you either explanations or drills. Bose Academy puts both in a single mission.

  1. Learn

    A short explanation of the idea, in plain language, before you practice.

  2. Practice

    Unlimited problems adjust after every answer, and each one has a tutor beside it.

  3. Check

    A spaced retention check, days later, confirms you still know it.

  4. Advance

    Your knowledge map shows what you've mastered and what to learn next.

Progress that rewards sticking with it

Your progress bar only goes up. One wrong answer never erases what you've learned, and when something is tricky, Bose Academy steps back to the building blocks instead of drilling harder.

A tutor that asks, not tells

When you're stuck, the tutor offers a nudge, then a clearer hint, then the first step. You do the thinking, so it stays with you.

A map of what you know

See every skill, how they connect, and the shortest path to your goal, from fractions to calculus.
